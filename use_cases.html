Use Cases

Where trust in a model decides whether it ships.

The same question comes up in every field: what did the model rely on, can we change it safely, and will it hold? Here is where Inverse AI starts, and where it goes next.

Published study
Healthcare

Medical imaging

“Did the model grade the retina, or the camera’s dark border?”

  • Explanations scored inside the anatomy that can carry evidence
  • Comparison of explanation methods per image and per grade
  • A per-image report a clinician can review

Starting point: diabetic retinopathy (Sight). A research study, not a medical device; any clinical use would need its own validation and regulatory approval.

In research
AI labs & product teams

Language-model safety and alignment

“We steered the model to be more careful. Did it work, compared with what, and what did it cost?”

  • STEER cards for every intervention
  • Evaluation against strong baselines, not just the untouched model
  • Capability and compute cost reported with each gain
In research
Multimodal AI

Vision-language models

“Does a change made for text still hold when the model also sees?”

  • One evaluation standard for text-only and vision-language models
  • Explicit labels where a result is untested in one modality
  • Image-side explanations checked the same way as in Sight
Planned
Finance · insurance · public services

Regulated decisions

“Can we show an auditor why this decision was made, and that the model hasn’t drifted since?”

  • Decision reports pairing each output with checked explanations
  • Explanation drift and fairness monitoring
  • An audit trail aligned with high-risk AI obligations
