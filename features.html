Features

What the platform is designed to do.

Inverse AI is pre-product. This page describes the capabilities we are building, grouped by layer, and marks how far each one has come.

Published method In research Planned
Inspect

Explanations you can check.

Most explanation tools stop at the picture. Inspect scores it: does the credit fall where evidence can exist, and do different methods agree?

Mask-aware explanation scoring

Published

Score Grad-CAM, SHAP and other attribution maps on whether credit falls inside the region that can carry evidence, such as the retinal disc, and not on borders or background.

Method from Sight

Method comparison per decision

Published

Run several explainers on the same prediction and show where they agree, where they split, and which grades or classes each handles better.

Method from Sight

Lesion-level validation

In research

Score explanations against lesion outlines where datasets provide them, so a heatmap is tested against the pathology and not only the anatomy.

Decision report

In design

One page per prediction with the output, the explanations, their scores, and a plain statement of what was and wasn’t measured.

Intervene

Change behaviour without retraining.

Representation steering edits what a running model does while its weights stay fixed. Intervene makes each edit legible, comparable, and costed.

Answer, clarify, or refuse

Prototype

A learned controller sits around an unchanged language model and decides, step by step, whether to reply, check with a second safety model, or refuse, and whether to send a draft once it is written.

Prototype: Toxicity Guard

STEER intervention cards

In research

Every steering change is written up the same way: Signal, Target site, Edit, Execution, Result. Two interventions can then be compared line by line.

Standard from Steer

Strong-baseline evaluation

In research

Each intervention is measured against the untouched model and against a strong alternative such as prompting or fine-tuning. Beating a weak baseline doesn’t count.

Cost accounting

In research

Report what the change cost in general capability, compute, and latency, next to what it gained.

Text and multimodal coverage

In research

The same standard applies to models that read and models that read and see. A result tested on one and not the other is labelled that way.

Monitor

Keep the evidence in production.

A model that behaved in the lab can drift once it’s live. Monitor carries the explanations and interventions into deployment, building on the founder’s published MLOps work.

Model CI/CD

In design

Versioned data, models, and pipelines, with continuous training and deployment. Every release can be traced and reproduced.

Builds on Information, 2020

Explanation drift

Planned

Alert when the reasons behind predictions shift, not only when accuracy drops.

Fairness and subgroup checks

Planned

Track performance and explanation quality across patient or user groups, release over release.

Audit trail

Planned

Log every model, explanation, and intervention for review against frameworks such as the EU AI Act.
