Roadmap

From published research to a platform teams can rely on.

We build in the order the evidence allows: methods first, then open tools and pilots, then the platform.

Now

Research foundation

  • Mask-aware explainability benchmark for diabetic retinopathy, published with IEEE (2026)
  • MLOps foundation: continuous automation for ML, published in Information (2020)
  • Toxicity Guard prototype: a reinforcement-learning controller that decides when a language model should answer, clarify, or refuse (2026)
  • Steering account for language and vision-language models, manuscript in preparation
  • Platform architecture and service repositories
  • Company formation and first funding round
Next

Open tools and first pilots

  • Open-source toolkit for scoring explanations, released from the Sight methods
  • Lesion-level validation of retinal explanations
  • STEER cards and a strong-baseline evaluation harness for steering
  • First pilot with a clinical or industry design partner
Later

The platform

  • Decision reports that pair each prediction with checked explanations
  • Runtime intervention with cost accounting
  • Production monitoring: explanation drift, fairness, audit trail
  • Evidence packs for high-risk AI compliance
